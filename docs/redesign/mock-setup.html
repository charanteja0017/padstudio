About

PadStudio 0.1 is free software under GPL-3.0. It builds on:

  • Key Mapper (GPL-3.0) runs the pen’s buttons from PadStudio’s profiles.
  • Lawnchair’s flowerpot (GPL-3.0-or-later) sorts apps into groups; its icon-pack handling guided PadStudio’s.
  • FocusPen Pro X (GPL-3.0-only) showed how HyperOS reports pen gestures.
  • Lawnicons (Apache-2.0) and Arcticons (GPL-3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0) supply themed icons.
  • Material Symbols (Apache-2.0): the icons.
  • model-viewer (Apache-2.0): the live 3D pen in this preview.
