PadStudio kit

Every shared component of the PadStudio 2 preview, drawn on your tablet's screen: 1164 × 777 dp in landscape, 1 dp = 1 px before the frame is scaled to fit. The switches change every frame at once. They start at your tablet's settings: dark, Violet, text 125 %, Bold text on.

A design preview, not a pixel match: Roboto Flex stands in for MiSans. Text sizes follow Android's own nonlinear font-scale tables (AOSP FontScaleConverterFactory.java, commit 64130047), so large text grows less than small text, and Bold text adds 300 to every weight. Numbers marked "example" are not readings from your tablet; "6 × 4" is the home-screen grid recorded on 11 Oct. App icons are your own, cut from your home-screen screenshots; the widget frames use sample tiles so that no app appears twice.
